🫧 Game Rules & Scoring Mechanics

🎯
Aim & Ricochet: Move your mouse or drag your finger to aim the pointer. Use side-wall bounces to reach high, tucked-away bubbles.
💥
Match-3 Cluster: Match 3 or more bubbles of the same color to pop them. Each popped bubble awards points and builds your combo.
💧
Floater Drops: Any bubbles cut off from the ceiling will drop and award huge cascade multipliers. Clear the anchors!
⚠️
Ceiling Descends: Every 5 shots without a pop, the ceiling shifts down. Prevent bubbles from touching the bottom dashed red line.

Desktop Controls: Move mouse to aim, Left Click to fire. Press S to swap bubbles and P to Pause.
Mobile Controls: Drag finger across the battlefield to position the trajectory line, then lift finger to fire.
